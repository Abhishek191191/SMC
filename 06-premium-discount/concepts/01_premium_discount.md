# 01: Premium & Discount: Wholesale vs Retail Pricing

## The Problem
Retail traders chase price. They see a massive green candle and they buy it, fearing they will miss the move. What they don't realize is they are buying at the absolute peak of the market. They are paying a massive premium. Institutions never pay retail prices.

## The Story: The Car Dealership
Would you go to a car dealership and offer to pay 20% over the sticker price? No. You wait for a sale. You negotiate. You want a discount. Institutions operate the exact same way. If they want to be long the Euro, they will not buy it after it has just rallied 300 pips. They will wait for a pullback into a "Discount" before deploying capital.

## The Concept
Premium and Discount (P/D) arrays are derived from the Dealing Range. By drawing a Fibonacci retracement from the Swing Low to the Swing High, we divide the range in half at the 50% Equilibrium level.
- **Premium:** Above 50%. This is expensive. You look for shorts here.
- **Discount:** Below 50%. This is cheap. You look for longs here.

### Why It Works
Institutions operate on strict algorithmic constraints regarding average entry price. They are mathematically forbidden from initiating new long campaigns in Premium pricing. They must drive the price down into Discount to accumulate.

## How It Works
1. **Identify Dealing Range:** Find the clear Swing Low and Swing High.
2. **Pull the Fib:** Mark the 50% Equilibrium.
3. **Wait for Alignment:** If you are bullish, you MUST wait for price to drop below the 50% line. Once in Discount, you look for a bullish Order Block or FVG to enter.

## Key Takeaways
- **Never buy in Premium. Never sell in Discount.** This one rule filters out 80% of bad trades.
- **Confluence:** An Order Block in a Discount zone is extremely high probability. An Order Block in a Premium zone (if you are bullish) is a trap.
