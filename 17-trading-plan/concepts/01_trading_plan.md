# 01: The Trading Plan: Your Business Mandate

## The Problem
"I trade whatever looks good." This trader is guaranteed to fail. Without a written plan, you are vulnerable to the whims of the market and your own emotions. You are a hobbyist, not a professional.

## The Story: The Franchise Operations Manual
If you buy a McDonald's franchise, they don't just hand you the keys and say "make burgers." They give you a 500-page operations manual detailing exactly how long to cook the fries, how to sweep the floors, and how to balance the register. A Trading Plan is your operations manual.

## The Concept
A Trading Plan is a comprehensive, rigid document outlining your precise methodology, risk parameters, routine, and psychological protocols.

### Why It Works
In the heat of the moment, when your heart rate is elevated, your IQ drops. The Trading Plan acts as an objective third-party manager. You don't have to make decisions live; you just execute the flowchart.

## How It Works (Core Components)
1. **Asset Class & Timeframes:** Exactly what you trade and when.
2. **Setup Criteria:** "If A, B, and C happen, I will execute D."
3. **Risk Model:** Max risk per trade, max daily loss limit.
4. **Routine:** Pre-market analysis, execution window, post-market review.

## Key Takeaways
- **If it's not written down, it's not a rule.**
- **The plan must be followed flawlessly. A profitable trade that broke your rules is a bad trade.**
