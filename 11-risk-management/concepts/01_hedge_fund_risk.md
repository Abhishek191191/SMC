# 01: Hedge Fund Risk: The Mathematics of Survival

## The Problem
Retail traders think about how much money they can make. Professional traders think about how much money they can lose. If you risk 5% per trade and hit a 10-trade losing streak (which is statistically inevitable), you lose 50% of your account. To recover from a 50% drawdown, you don't need to make 50%—you need to make 100%.

## The Story: The Casino vs The Gambler
The gambler goes to the casino, bets his whole paycheck on black, and hopes for luck. The casino doesn't rely on luck. They rely on a 1.5% mathematical edge in Roulette. Over millions of spins, that tiny edge guarantees billions in profit. The casino rigorously manages risk by imposing table maximums so no single gambler can wipe them out. You must be the casino.

## The Concept
Risk management is the mathematical framework that ensures you survive long enough for your trading edge to play out over a large sample size.

### Why It Works
Variance (the random distribution of wins and losses) is a law of probability. Even with a 60% win rate strategy, you can easily experience 8 losses in a row. Proper position sizing ensures that this inevitable string of losses is just a minor dip in your equity curve, not an account blower.

## How It Works
1. **Fixed Fractional Risk:** Always risk a fixed percentage of your account per trade (e.g., 1%). If your account grows, your absolute risk grows. If you take losses, your absolute risk shrinks, preserving capital.
2. **R-Multiples:** Stop thinking in dollars. Think in R (Risk). If you risk $100 to make $300, that is a 3R trade.
3. **Expectancy:** The mathematical formula that tells you if your system prints money or burns it. `(Win Rate * Average Win) - (Loss Rate * Average Loss)`.

## Key Takeaways
- **Capital preservation is the absolute priority.**
- **You are trading a portfolio of setups over time, not a single outcome.**
