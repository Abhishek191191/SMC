# 01: The Path to Professionalism & Prop Firms

## The Problem
A trader finally achieves a profitable 3-month track record on a $1,000 account. They decide to quit their job and trade full-time. They immediately blow the account due to the immense psychological pressure of needing the market to pay their rent.

## The Story: The Minor Leagues to the Majors
A baseball player hitting home runs in the minor leagues doesn't immediately get thrown into the World Series. They scale up gradually. They get accustomed to the faster pitches and the bigger crowds. In trading, scaling capital too quickly is psychological suicide.

## The Concept
Transitioning to professional trading requires navigating the psychological shift from paper trading/small capital to handling massive institutional or prop-firm capital.

### Why It Works
Proprietary trading firms (Prop Firms) allow retail traders to access large capital ($100k - $1M+) by passing an evaluation. However, these evaluations are designed to fail traders with poor risk management. The skills required to pass a prop firm challenge are different from growing a personal account.

## How It Works
1. **The Prop Firm Model:** Understanding max daily drawdowns, trailing drawdowns, and consistency rules.
2. **Capital Scaling:** Moving from Demo -> Small Live -> Prop Firm Challenge -> Funded Account -> Multiple Funded Accounts.
3. **The Professional Routine:** Treating trading like a 9-5 business, complete with HR (Psychology) and Accounting (Journaling) departments.

## Key Takeaways
- **Trading is a profession, not a get-rich-quick scheme.**
- **Protecting capital is more important than making capital.**
