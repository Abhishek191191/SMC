# 01: Trading Psychology: Decision Making Under Uncertainty

## The Problem
You backtest a strategy and it works flawlessly. You trade it live, and you lose money. You hesitate on valid setups, you move your stop loss because "it just needs a little more room," and you revenge trade after a loss. Your edge isn't the problem; your mind is.

## The Story: The Lizard Brain
Humans evolved to survive on the savannah. Our brains are hardwired for "fight or flight" when facing danger (a lion, or a red candle). We are hardwired to seek immediate gratification (finding berries, or taking a quick 1R profit instead of waiting for 3R). The market requires the exact opposite of our evolutionary programming. You have to learn to override your lizard brain.

## The Concept
Trading psychology is not about suppressing emotion; it is about establishing rigid frameworks and processes that prevent emotions from dictating actions.

### Why It Works
The market is a mirror. It exposes your deep-seated flaws: impatience, greed, fear of being wrong, ego. Professionals succeed because they detach their ego from the outcome of a single trade. They think in probabilities.

## How It Works
1. **Accepting Risk:** Truly accepting that a trade can lose *before* you place it. If you are anxious during a trade, your position size is too big.
2. **Process over Outcome:** Judge yourself not by whether the trade won or lost, but by whether you followed your plan flawlessly. A perfectly executed losing trade is a good trade.
3. **Emotional Regulation:** Stepping away from the screens after a significant loss (or a massive win) to reset your dopamine baseline.

## Key Takeaways
- **You cannot control the market; you can only control yourself.**
- **Think in probabilities, not certainties.**
