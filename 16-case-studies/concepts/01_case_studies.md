# 01: Case Studies: The Anatomy of a Trade

## The Problem
Theory is clean; real markets are messy. Reading about an Order Block is easy. Identifying one in real-time, amidst news spikes, conflicting timeframe biases, and imperfect structure, is incredibly difficult.

## The Story: The Surgeon's Autopsy
Medical students spend years reading textbooks, but they learn the most by observing real surgeries and performing autopsies. A case study is an autopsy of a trade. We dissect the exact anatomy of what went right, what went wrong, and the nuances that textbooks miss.

## The Concept
Case studies bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application by analyzing historical trades with full top-down, multi-timeframe context.

### Why It Works
By studying massive winners and painful losers, you build pattern recognition. You train your eye to see the confluence of Time, Price, and Liquidity.

## How It Works (The Case Study Framework)
1. **The HTF Narrative (Daily/4H):** Establishing the bias and draw on liquidity.
2. **The Setup (15m):** Identifying the specific institutional footprints (OBs/FVGs).
3. **The Execution (1m):** The microscopic entry logic (CHoCH, spread allowance).
4. **The Management:** How the trade was trailed and exited.
5. **The Post-Mortem:** What could have been done better?

## Key Takeaways
- **Study your losers more than your winners.** Winners stroke your ego; losers pay your tuition.
