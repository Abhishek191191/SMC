# 01: Exit Strategies: Targets & Trailing

## The Problem
"I was up 3R, and it came all the way back to stop me out at breakeven." This is the anthem of the retail trader. They spend 90% of their time finding entries and 10% of their time planning exits.

## The Story: The Heist
Imagine a perfectly planned bank heist (the entry). The crew gets into the vault and bags the cash. But they didn't plan an escape route (the exit). They stay in the vault too long, the police arrive, and they lose everything. A trade is only a theoretical win until you click "close."

## The Concept
Exits must be as systematic as entries. You must know exactly where you are taking partial profits and where your final target is *before* you enter the trade.

### Why It Works
The market algorithm seeks liquidity. Therefore, your take profit targets should be resting exactly where the market is trying to go: the opposing side's liquidity pools.

## How It Works
1. **The Target:** If you are long from a Discount Order Block, your primary target should be the nearest major Premium Liquidity pool (BSL / Equal Highs).
2. **Taking Partials:** Securing 50% or 80% of your position at a logical intermediate roadblock (e.g., a massive FVG). This secures cash and protects psychology.
3. **Trailing Stops:** Moving the stop loss up behind newly formed structural higher lows. *Warning: Retail trails stops too tightly and gets swept. Only trail behind confirmed external structure.*

## Key Takeaways
- **Never let a full target winner turn into a loser.** Manage risk aggressively once price clears the first major hurdle.
- **Targets are magnets.** Don't target random numbers; target the liquidity.
