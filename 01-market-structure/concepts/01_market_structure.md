# 01: Market Structure: Beyond Higher Highs

## The Problem
Retail traders look at structure as a rigid geometry. They see a higher high and assume "up trend." But they don't ask *why* the high was broken. Was it broken with institutional displacement, or was it a liquidity sweep (a stop hunt) designed to look like a breakout? This is why retail buys the breakout and immediately gets stopped out.

## The Story: The Advancing Army
Think of market structure like an advancing army. They push forward, establish a base camp (higher low), and then push forward again to conquer new territory (higher high). If they push too far without establishing a strong base camp, their supply lines stretch, and they are easily overrun by the enemy. A true break of structure requires committed volume (infantry), not just a quick scouting mission (a wick).

## The Concept
Market Structure in SMC is the framework for reading institutional intent. It consists of Swing Highs, Swing Lows, Break of Structure (BOS), and Change of Character (CHoCH).

### Why It Works
Structure works because it maps the path of least resistance. Institutions will continue to push price in a direction until they encounter massive opposing liquidity. A BOS confirms they are continuing the campaign. A CHoCH suggests they have hit a wall and are reversing to seek liquidity in the opposite direction.

## How It Works: Internal vs External
1. **External Structure:** The macro swing points on a higher timeframe (e.g., Daily chart). This shows the overarching institutional bias.
2. **Internal Structure:** The micro swings inside the external leg (e.g., 15m chart). This shows the intraday order flow.
3. **The Trap:** Retail trades internal structure blindly, forgetting they are trading directly into a major external institutional defense point.

## Key Takeaways
- **Structure is intent.** A true BOS with full body candle closure shows institutional commitment. A wick shows rejection and liquidity sweeping.
- **Fractal nature.** Every internal trend is just one leg of an external structure. Aligning them is the key to high probability.
