Visualizing the Institutional Cycle

Why This Matters: This cycle represents how liquidity is engineered. Institutions cannot participate without this precise sequence of events.

[ Consolidation ]

Retail traders buy support and sell resistance. Stop losses pool above and below.

⬇

[ Manipulation (Stop Hunt) ]

Price breaks support. Retail breakout traders sell. Retail range traders' stops are hit (also sells).
Institution buys into this sell pressure.

⬇

[ Distribution ]

Institution has their position. Price rallies to the opposing liquidity pool to take profit.

What You're Seeing

This is not a pattern to memorize; it is the physical requirement of a large order being placed in an auction market. The manipulation phase is the whale entering the pool slowly, using the water (liquidity) displaced by retail traders hitting their stops.
